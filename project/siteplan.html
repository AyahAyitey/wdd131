1. Site Name

StudyNest

StudyNest is the chosen name for this project website. The word Study immediately signals the site's academic focus, while Nest evokes a comfortable, organized space — a home base for learning. Together, the name communicates a welcoming environment where students can gather resources, track progress, and stay motivated.

Optional domain availability: studynest.info — short, memorable, and directly tied to the site's purpose.

2. Site Purpose

StudyNest is an online resource hub designed for college and high-school students who want to improve their study habits and academic performance. The site will provide:

  • Curated study-skill guides (time management, note-taking methods, exam strategies)
  • A subject-resource directory linking to free textbooks, tutorials, and practice tools
  • A motivational quote and tip feed to keep students engaged
  • A simple contact form for visitors to suggest new resources or ask questions

The overall goal is to be a one-stop reference that students return to regularly throughout the semester for both practical help and inspiration.

3. Scenarios

The following scenario questions represent real visitors in the target audience. They guide what content must be present and easy to find on the site.

Scenario 1 – The Overwhelmed Freshman

"I just started college and I have four exams in one week. Where can I find a proven study schedule or time-management method that actually works for college-level courses?"

Scenario 2 – The Resource Hunter

"I can't afford the required textbook for my Biology class. Does this site list any free or low-cost alternatives, like open-access textbooks or video lecture series, that cover the same material?"

Scenario 3 – The Returning Visitor

"I visited StudyNest last week and read a great article on the Pomodoro technique. Is there new content added this week, and is there a way to get notified about new study tips?"

4. Color Scheme

The palette balances professionalism with warmth. Navy anchors the brand with authority and calm, while amber provides energy and draws attention to key calls to action.

#1a3c5e Deep Navy Blue — Header, nav, headings, footer
#e8a020 Warm Amber — Accents, borders, hover states
#f5f7fa Light Gray-Blue — Page background
#ffffff White — Section card backgrounds
#2d2d2d Near-Black — Body text, paragraphs

5. Typography

Two Google Fonts are used to create a clear visual hierarchy:

Merriweather (Serif) – Headings

Used for all h1–h3 elements and the site name in the header. Its classic serif style conveys credibility and academic seriousness.

The Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over the Lazy Dog

Merriweather Regular — Page headings, section titles

Open Sans (Sans-Serif) – Body Text

Used for all paragraph text, navigation links, captions, and list items. Its clean, humanist letterforms make long passages easy to read on any screen size.

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.

Open Sans Regular — Body copy, navigation, captions, labels
