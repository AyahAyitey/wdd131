Trailrunning.com

A practical, welcoming trail-running guide for curious beginners and experienced runners looking for useful route, gear, and training information.

Purpose and audience

Trailrunning.com gathers approachable trail-running information in one place so new participants can discover routes, prepare thoughtfully, and build fitness at a sustainable pace. The primary audience is people curious about getting off the road; the site also supports experienced runners who want quick reference information.

Planned content

  • Home: introduce trail running and direct visitors to routes, gear, and training.
  • Trail finder: show routes with location, distance, elevation gain, and difficulty; offer interactive filters.
  • Gear guide: explain useful equipment and how to choose it for the outing.
  • Training: share beginner-friendly guidance and an interactive weekly starting-point planner.
  • References: credit factual sources and photography.

Visual direction

Editorial outdoor field-guide aesthetic: deep evergreen, bright citrus, warm white, and restrained clay accents. Manrope headings and DM Sans body text keep the site clear and contemporary. Large landscape photography signals the outdoors; compact route information is organized for quick scanning.

Accessibility and interaction

Semantic landmarks, keyboard-visible focus, skip link, labeled forms, descriptive page titles and meta descriptions, responsive layouts, and meaningful image alternatives support access. JavaScript provides route filtering, saved route favorites, a mobile navigation toggle, and a locally saved training planner.
