About Japan

Japan is an island nation in East Asia situated in the northwest Pacific Ocean. It comprises four main islands – Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, and Shikoku – and more than 6,800 smaller islands. With a population of approximately 125 million, Japan blends ancient traditions with cutting-edge modernity.

From the neon-lit streets of Tokyo to the serene bamboo groves of Arashiyama, Japan offers an extraordinary diversity of experiences for every traveler.

Highlights

  • 🏯 Osaka Castle – A symbol of Japan's feudal past and one of its most iconic landmarks.
  • 🌸 Cherry Blossoms (Sakura) – Every spring Japan bursts into breathtaking pink and white blooms.
  • 🍣 Japanese Cuisine – From sushi and ramen to wagyu beef and matcha everything.
  • 🗻 Mount Fuji – Japan's highest peak and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
